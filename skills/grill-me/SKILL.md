Interview me relentlessly about this plan until we reach a shared understanding.

- If `DOMAIN.md` exists, read it first and use its terms. Flag when I contradict an invariant or a past decision.
- One question at a time, each with your recommended answer.
- Resolve decisions others depend on first.
- If the codebase or `DOMAIN.md` answers it, look it up instead of asking.
- When done, list: decisions, domain changes, open questions. Don't edit `DOMAIN.md`; that's `/craft-domain`'s job.
